Unlocked

This looks like click-lock LVP over a concrete slab. Surface wear is light, but the photo shows edge peaking in one area. Refinishing isn't realistic for LVP — plan on replace. Ballpark for a typical bedroom: about three to six dollars per square foot installed, more if the subfloor needs leveling. Have a pro confirm moisture under the slab before you buy new flooring.

  • Type confirmation: click-lock LVP (luxury vinyl plank)
  • Condition: light surface wear; edge peaking in one area
  • Refinish vs replace: replace — LVP cannot be refinished like hardwood
  • Cost band: roughly $3–$6 / sq ft installed for a typical bedroom; more if leveling is needed
  • Subfloor red flags: concrete slab — check moisture before new install
  • Pro checklist: moisture test under slab, flatness/leveling, product waterproof rating, transition pieces, underlayment compatibility
