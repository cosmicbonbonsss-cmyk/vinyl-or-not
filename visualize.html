Which flooring look?

Tap a look to swap the floor. On a sample room it updates right away. On your photo, mark the four corners first. Every option uses a floor image on this site.

Measure & estimate

Scale depends on your reference measurement and a flat-floor assumption. Perspective warp means this is a rough estimate — not a bid.

Mark the floor first. For boxes and planks to order, use the Measure calculator — this panel is only a rough area from the photo.

Known limits
  • Sample rooms are pre-marked. Your own photo still needs four taps — the floor is not detected for you.
  • Camera help only checks blur and brightness. It does not identify the floor.
  • Furniture, rugs, and uneven floors won’t be masked. The overlay is a flat four-corner stretch.
  • Lighting in the room is not matched. Extreme angles can soften the plank lines.
  • Best with a clear view of a mostly open rectangular floor.
