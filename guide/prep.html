Chapter 5

Prep the room

Get the subfloor flat, dry, and clean before how to install vinyl plank flooring begins. Click-lock forgives small mistakes in tapping — it does not forgive a wavy, wet, or dirty subfloor. Prep is where DIY jobs succeed or fail.

Clear and protect

  • Remove baseboards carefully if you plan to reuse them (label pieces).
  • Haul out furniture; protect adjacent rooms from dust.
  • Decide whether old flooring stays (only if the manufacturer allows it — and it’s flat, sound, and thin enough).

Flat, dry, clean

  1. Flatness — many brands want roughly 3/16 in over 10 ft (confirm your sheet). Grind high spots; fill low spots with leveling compound rated for your substrate.
  2. Moisture — concrete slabs need proper testing (surface “looks dry” is not enough). Wood subfloors should be sound, screwed down, and within moisture limits.
  3. Clean — scrape adhesive ridges, sweep, vacuum. Debris telegraphs through thin LVP.
  4. Underlayment / vapor barrier — install only what the brand specifies. Skip a second pad if foam is already attached.
  5. Acclimate — follow the box: some rigid cores need little time; others want 48 hours in the room.
Red flags — pause: soft/spongy spots, musty smell, mold, large cracks with height change, recent flooding without a documented dry-out. Get a pro opinion before you open boxes.
