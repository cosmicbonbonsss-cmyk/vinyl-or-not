    Guide · LVP cost

    Quick answer

    How much does vinyl plank (LVP) flooring cost per square foot?

    Roughly $1.50 to $7 per square foot for the planks, and roughly $4 to $12 per square foot installed by a pro. Most mid-grade installed jobs land around $5 to $9. These are approximate 2026 US ranges from national cost guides. Your local quotes and store prices will differ.

    The short version

    • Planks only: about $1.50 to $7 per sq ft. The wear layer and brand drive most of the spread.
    • Pro labor: often about $2 to $5 per sq ft for click-lock. Glue-down, stairs, and choppy rooms cost more.
    • Installed total: about $4 to $12 per sq ft, before tear-out and subfloor repairs.
    • DIY: you skip labor, but still pay for waste, trim, transitions, and a few tools.

    First, get your real square footage. The free measure calculator adds waste and rounds up to full boxes, so every number below has something to multiply against.

    Underlayment

    • Attached pad: $0 extra. Most brands say not to add a second pad under it.
    • Separate underlayment: about $0.25–$1 per sq ft. Sound-rated or moisture-barrier rolls cost more.
    • Over concrete: check whether your brand wants a vapor barrier, even with an attached pad.

    See Prep for which kind your floor needs.

    Tools for a DIY job

    A click-lock room needs only a few tools. Many people already own a tape measure, square, and utility knife.

    • Install kit (tapping block, pull bar, spacers, mallet): about $20–$35.
    • Vinyl plank cutter: about $40–$130 to buy, or rent one. A utility knife works fine for a small room.
    • Jamb saw or oscillating tool: for undercutting door casings. Borrow or rent if you can.
    • Knee pads: cheap, and worth it.

    DIY vs pro labor

    Labor is often a third to half of an installed LVP bill. Here is roughly what you pay for it.

    DIY

    $0 labor. You pay for planks plus about 10% waste, any underlayment, trim, transitions, and tools. You also spend a weekend or two, depending on the rooms. Mistakes cost planks.

    Pro install

    Click-lock labor is often about $2–$5 per sq ft. Angi's 2026 range for vinyl install labor is wider, about $1–$10, because it covers glue-down, stairs, and tricky rooms. Small rooms may hit a minimum job charge.

    Extras people forget

    Removing the old floor

    Often about $1–$2 per sq ft when a pro does it. Carpet is on the cheap end. Glued vinyl and tile cost more.

    Old vinyl or glue in an older home can contain asbestos. Test before you scrape.

    Subfloor prep

    Leveling or patching is often about $1–$3 per sq ft where it's needed. Replacing a damaged subfloor costs much more.

    Check flatness before you get quotes. See Prep.

    Quarter round or baseboard

    Primed quarter round is about $1–$2 per linear foot at Home Depot and Lowe's. Installed, quotes often run about $1.50–$4 per foot.

    Reusing your baseboards saves the most.

    Transitions

    T-moldings and reducers are often about $10–$30 each, or more for matching colors. A pro may charge about $25–$75 per doorway installed.

    One at each doorway where the floor changes.

    Angi suggests budgeting about $150–$700 extra for subfloor repairs, tear-out, and transitions on a typical vinyl job. Furniture moving, stairs, and disposal fees are often separate lines too.

    Worked example: a 12×12 bedroom

    A 12 ft × 12 ft room is 144 sq ft. Add about 10% waste for cuts and you need about 159 sq ft. With boxes of about 20 sq ft, that is 8 boxes, or 160 sq ft. Your box size will differ, so run your own room through the measure calculator.

    1. Planks (160 sq ft): budget about $240–$400, mid-range about $400–$640, premium 20 mil about $640–$1,120.
    2. Underlayment: $0 with an attached pad, or about $40–$145 for a separate one.
    3. Quarter round: six 8 ft pieces (48 ft) is about $50–$100 in materials.
    4. One transition at the door: about $10–$30.
    5. Tools: about $20–$35 for an install kit. A cutter is optional.

    DIY, mid-range planks

    About $500–$950 total. That works out to roughly $3.30–$6.60 per sq ft of floor.

    Pro, mid-range planks

    About $800–$1,750. That is the planks plus about $2–$5 per sq ft labor, installed trim, and a transition. Add about $150–$300 if they also pull up the carpet.

    Rough math, not a quote. Tax, delivery, a minimum job charge, or subfloor repairs can move it.

    Ways to save

    • Measure first. Buy about 10% extra, not 25%. Leftover boxes are wasted money, and running short can mean a different dye lot.
    • Tear out the old floor yourself. Carpet and pad removal is easy DIY work, and it can take $1–$2 per sq ft off a quote.
    • Use the attached pad. If the planks have one, you usually don't need separate underlayment.
    • Reuse baseboards and add quarter round instead of buying all-new trim.
    • Get three itemized quotes so you can see labor, removal, prep, and trim as separate lines.
    • Watch for sales on in-stock colors, and rent the specialty tools.
    • Don't cut the wear layer to save money in rooms with dogs or heavy traffic. Replacing a worn floor costs more.

    When a pro is worth it

    • The subfloor is out of flat, damaged, or has had moisture problems.
    • Stairs (see LVP on stairs for cost per step), glue-down planks, or rooms with lots of doorways and angles.
    • Bathrooms where the toilet has to come up, or kitchens with appliances to move.
    • Large open areas that need careful layout in one go.
    • Old vinyl or glue that might contain asbestos.
    • You need it done fast, or you want a labor warranty.

    For a single simple bedroom on a flat subfloor, DIY usually makes sense. See the install quick start to judge whether it is a job for you.

    LVP cost FAQ

    How much does vinyl plank flooring cost per square foot?

    Roughly $1.50 to $7 per square foot for the planks alone, depending on wear layer and brand, and roughly $4 to $12 per square foot installed by a pro. Most mid-grade installed jobs land around $5 to $9. These are approximate 2026 US ranges from national cost guides, and local prices vary.

    How much is labor to install LVP?

    Click-lock LVP labor is often quoted around $2 to $5 per square foot. Angi's 2026 range for vinyl installation labor is wider, about $1 to $10, because it covers glue-down, stairs, and complex rooms. Small rooms may hit a minimum job charge. Removal and subfloor prep are usually extra.

    Is it cheaper to install LVP yourself?

    Usually, yes. DIY skips the labor line, which is often a third to half of the bill. You still pay for planks, waste, underlayment if needed, trim, transitions, and a few tools. Mistakes cost planks, so buy about 10% extra.

    What wear layer should I buy if I have dogs?

    Start at 20 mil. About 6 mil is for light use and about 12 mil is the usual home floor. A 20 mil wear layer costs more per square foot but holds up better to claws and grit.

    What extra costs should I budget for besides planks and labor?

    Underlayment if the planks have no attached pad (about $0.25 to $1 per square foot), removing the old floor (often about $1 to $2 per square foot), subfloor leveling (about $1 to $3 per square foot where needed), quarter round or baseboard, and transition strips at doorways.
