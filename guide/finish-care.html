Chapter 7

Finish & care

Once the planks are down, trim, transitions, and gentle cleaning make the floor look finished — and help it last.

Finish the edges

  • Remove spacers; reinstall baseboards or add matching quarter-round. Nail into the wall, not the floating floor.
  • Set transition strips so they cover the expansion gap but don’t pin the floor tightly.
  • Caulk only where the brand allows (often wall/trim junctions — not locking seams).

First-week care

  • Furniture: lift, don’t drag. Use felt pads.
  • Rolling chairs: use a floor mat rated for vinyl.
  • Clean up grit — sand acts like sandpaper on wear layers.

Ongoing cleaning

  • Soft broom or vacuum (no beater bar digging into embossing).
  • Damp mop with cleaner approved for vinyl — avoid steam mops and soaking water.
  • Skip wax/polish unless the manufacturer says otherwise (most modern wear layers don’t want it).
Keep one sealed carton of leftover planks for future repairs. Dye lots change.
