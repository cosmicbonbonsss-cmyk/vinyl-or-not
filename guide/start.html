Chapter 1

Start here

Before you dive into how to install vinyl plank flooring, figure out what you already have and which site tool answers your next question. Five minutes here can save a wrong buy later.

1. Identify what’s on the floor now

Use the free photo report on the home page: snap 1–3 close-ups of seams, edges, and texture, then answer a short checklist. You get a plain-English guess (click-lock LVP vs tile vs wood, for example) — not AI vision and not a licensed inspection.

Open photo report — is it vinyl? →

2. Pick the DIY tool that fits

  • Still identifying? Stay on the photo report.
  • Ready to order sizes? Go to Measure for length × width and waste.
  • Choosing a look? Try Visualize to overlay planks on a room photo.
  • Want inspiration? Browse the before & after gallery.
This guide focuses on click-lock LVP for DIY. Glue-down vinyl shows up later as an advanced path — most homeowners should stick with floating click-lock.

3. Then follow the chapters in order

Understand the product → gather tools → measure → prep → install → finish → troubleshoot. Skip ahead only if you already know the earlier material.

Use with site tools

  • Photo report — identify flooring type from photos + checklist
  • Measure — estimate how much to buy
  • Visualize — preview LVP on your room photo
  • Projects — real before & after examples
