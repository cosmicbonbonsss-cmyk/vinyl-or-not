    Medium oak floor for a 10 by 12 kitchen

    A 10 by 12 kitchen is about 120 square feet. With 10% waste, plan on about 132 square feet of medium oak flooring — roughly 7 boxes at 20 square feet per box. Check the coverage on your carton with the measure tool before you order.
