Subfloor prep checklist — Vinyl or Not (planning aid)
=====================================================
Disclaimer: Planning aid only — not a manufacturer specification sheet
or warranty. Site conditions vary; follow product install guides and
local code.

Before ordering / install day
-----------------------------
[ ] Identify subfloor type: concrete slab, plywood/OSB, existing vinyl, etc.
[ ] Confirm product is approved over that substrate (and over existing floors
    if leaving them in place).
[ ] Check flatness: typically within ~3/16" in 10 ft (confirm brand limits).
[ ] Mark high/low spots; plan leveling compound or sanding as needed.
[ ] Moisture: concrete slabs need testing (see moisture-testing-notes.txt).
[ ] Remove debris, nails, adhesive ridges that would telegraph through.
[ ] Address squeaks / loose panels on wood subfloors.
[ ] Plan underlayment / vapor barrier per manufacturer.
[ ] Confirm radiant heat compatibility and max surface temperature if present.
[ ] Measure transitions: door clearances, reducers, T-moldings, stair nosing.
[ ] Protect adjacent finishes; plan for expansion gaps at walls and posts.

Red flags (pause and get a pro opinion)
---------------------------------------
- Soft, spongy, or water-stained areas
- Musty odor or visible mold
- Crumbling concrete or large cracks with height change
- Existing floor that is not well bonded
- Below-grade or slab-on-grade with no moisture history
