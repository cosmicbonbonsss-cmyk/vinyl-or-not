Waste factor guide — Vinyl or Not (planning aid)
================================================
Disclaimer: Planning aid only — not a manufacturer specification sheet
or contractor takeoff. Confirm quantities with your installer before
ordering, especially for stairs and irregular shapes.

What "waste" covers
-------------------
Cuts at walls, pattern matching, mistakes, damaged planks, and a small
attic stock for future repairs / matching dye lots.

Typical ranges (LVP / plank / tile)
-----------------------------------
- Simple rectangular rooms, straight lay: about 10%
- Moderate cuts, multiple rooms, doorways: about 10–12%
- Diagonal / herringbone / busy patterns, many angles: about 12–15%+
- Stairs, landings, and custom cuts: often separate — ask the installer

How to apply
------------
1. Measure each room (length × width) — see measure.html
2. Sum square footage
3. Multiply by (1 + waste%), e.g. 147 sq ft × 1.10 ≈ 162 sq ft
4. Round up to full boxes / cartons (never order exact bare feet)

Multi-unit / property managers
------------------------------
- Same product across units: order together when possible for dye-lot match
- Keep labeled attic stock per building or floor
- Slightly higher waste % if crews rotate or units have odd layouts

Designers
---------
- Pattern direction and feature walls change waste — note layout intent
  on the quote so the takeoff matches the presentation boards.
