    How to measure

    A tape and these steps are enough before you order.

    1. Type feet and inches in the calculator above. Measure the longest wall for length and the next wall for width. Round up to the next inch. You do not need to convert inches into decimals — 12 ft 6 in goes in as 12 feet and 6 inches.
    2. Add each extra space with “Add another area.” Closets that get the same floor count. Name the area so the list stays readable. Count a shared doorway once, not in both rooms.
    3. Leave waste at 10% for a simple rectangle. Use 15% for diagonals, lots of angles, or a busy pattern. The extra covers cuts, a mis-cut, and a few spare planks.
    4. Match box coverage and plank size to the carton. Many LVP boxes cover about 20 sq ft, but the number printed on your box is the one to use. Boxes and planks in the result are rounded up.
    5. Measure doorway openings separately if you need transition strips. Those strips are not part of the square footage.

    Simple example

    Bedroom: 12 ft × 11 ft = 132 sq ft

    Closet: 5 ft × 3 ft = 15 sq ft

    Subtotal = 132 + 15 = 147 sq ft

    Waste at 10%: 147 × 0.10 = 14.7 → round up to ~15 sq ft

    Waste at 15%: 147 × 0.15 ≈ 22 sq ft

    Order about 162–169 sq ft (147 + 10–15% waste). Buy a little extra if the product is a special order or may be discontinued.

    This is a planning aid only — not a contractor takeoff. Confirm measurements with your installer before ordering, especially for stairs, irregular shapes, and radiant heat or moisture-prone slabs.
