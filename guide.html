DIY path

Vinyl plank from zero to expert

A progressive path from “what is this floor?” to a confident click-lock install — written in plain English, matched to the free DIY tools on this site.

Mission

Help homeowners identify what they have, choose the right LVP for the room, gather tools, measure and prep honestly, then install click-lock flooring without guessing. Some jobs still need a hired installer (moisture, major leveling, baths/stairs) — this guide tells you when to pause.

How site DIY tools fit in

  • Photo report — upload photos + checklist to guess if it’s vinyl (Start here)
  • Measure — square footage and waste before you buy
  • Visualize — preview plank look on a room photo
  • Projects — before & after gallery for real-job inspiration
